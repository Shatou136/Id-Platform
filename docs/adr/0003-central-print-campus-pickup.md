# One central printer; pickup after Arrival at a Campus

SLUI has many campuses. There is one Evolis at a central desk. Admins may accept Requests from anywhere. Print happens on that office PC. The plastic is moved to the Campus outside the app. Staff mark Arrival, then the Student is told to pick up. Pinging at Print time would send students to an empty desk. Campus can change only before Print, because Campus is on the card.
